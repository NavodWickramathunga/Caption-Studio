Terms of service

What the service does

Caption Studio adds word-level captions to short videos. It can also write a script, speak it aloud, and time the captions against that recording. Editing, caption rendering and video export all happen inside your own browser.

Your account

You sign in with a Google account. You are responsible for what happens under your account. You must be old enough to form a binding contract where you live, and at least 13 in any case.

Your content stays yours

You keep all rights to the clips, scripts and videos you make. We claim no ownership over them and do not use them to train anything. We store only what is needed to run the service, as described in the privacy section.

What you must not do

  • Upload content you do not have the rights to use.
  • Make content that is unlawful, that harasses or defames someone, or that sexualises a minor.
  • Impersonate a real person using a generated voice, or use a synthetic voice to deceive.
  • Resell access, or run the service through automated tooling to get around usage limits.

We may suspend an account that does these things.

Usage limits

Generating speech and scripts costs us money per use, so there is a monthly allowance on each account. Reaching it stops further generation until the next month; nothing you have already made is affected. We may change the allowance, and will say so in the app before a reduction takes effect.

Advertising

The service is currently funded by advertising. Adverts are supplied by third parties who set their own cookies and collect their own data under their own policies.

Availability, and the honest limits

The service is provided as is. We do not guarantee it will be uninterrupted or error-free.

Two specific limits worth stating plainly, because they are properties of how it works rather than bugs:

  • Video export needs a recent Chrome or Edge. Safari, and therefore every iPhone and iPad, cannot export video here. This is a browser capability we do not control.
  • Rendering happens on your machine. Speed and success depend on your computer. An older machine may be slow, or may fail to build a 4K file.

What we do not hold

Your video and audio files are never uploaded to us. Clips stay on your computer, are read by your browser, and the finished file is written by your browser. We never receive them and could not produce one if asked.

We do not take payment details. We do not sell personal data to anyone.

Who else sees your script

When you use a feature that writes or speaks a script, the text of that script is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the result, and the audio comes back to you. That is the only place your script goes, and only when you press one of those buttons. Google processes it under its own API terms.

Our servers run on Google Cloud in Mumbai, India. Adverts are served by third-party networks with their own policies.

Cookies
